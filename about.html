About

Barranquilla Flavor is a tuition-free, community-based Latin and cultural dance program for children ages 6-17 in Concord, NH. Founded in 2018, Barranquilla Flavor celebrates the joy of dance from Colombia and around the world, seeking to bring diversity to community and multicultural events statewide and regionally throughout April to November.

Primarily in Latin dance, students perform a wide array of genres, including Cumbia, Mapale, Salsa, Merengue, Reggaeton, and Champeta. In addition, dancers have the opportunity to perform a cultural dance that represents their heritage.

Dancers learn and explore cultural dance while building confidence, leadership skills, and friendships.

Barranquilla Flavor is a tuition-free dance program that uses community involvement and donations to enable more children to experience the power and joy of dance.
